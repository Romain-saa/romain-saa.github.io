About Me

My Background

My name is Romain, I am 30 years old. I worked for 5 years as an ambulance driver, a profession deeply focused on human relations. This experience taught me how to manage critical situations, to stay calm under pressure, and to be an active listener. These are core values that I bring into the IT field today.

My Passion

At the same time, IT has always been my true calling. Since childhood, I have been fascinated by how computers work, spending my time exploring, testing, and tinkering. Naturally curious and encouraged by a circle of relatives working in this industry, I decided to turn this passion into a professional career.

My Evolution

Enrolling in the BTS SIO (Advanced Technician's Certificate in IT Services to Organizations) was a major turning point. Although I thought I had a good foundation, this training made me realize the vastness of the field. I acquired solid technical skills, but above all, I learned how to learn. Today, I am more motivated than ever to cultivate this thirst for knowledge to take on new challenges.
